## What is MegaETH?

MegaETH is a new EVM-compatible blockchain that calls itself "the first real-time blockchain." The key selling points:

- **Real-time execution** — Block times measured in milliseconds, not seconds
- **Gas is free** — During this testnet/mainnet hybrid phase
- **Local-first** — Designed for applications that need instant feedback

It's not fully mainnet yet — it's in this weird liminal state where real value exists (you can bridge real ETH) but gas costs are subsidized. That creates an interesting opportunity.

## The Opportunity: Potential Airdrop

MegaETH has a token (TGE = Token Generation Event) coming. The triggers:

- **$500M USDM in circulation**, OR
- **10 apps launched**, OR
- **3 apps earning $50K+/month**

Currently sitting at ~5 "Mafia apps" (per @waleswoosh). Not there yet, but growing.

The bet: early activity might get rewarded. Not guaranteed, but with free gas, the cost of being wrong is near zero.

## My Strategy

I'm not trying to game one protocol or maximize volume. Instead:

1. **Diverse interactions** — Touch as many protocols as possible
2. **Small amounts** — Keep risk low while establishing usage patterns
3. **Document everything** — Build a track record (and content)
4. **Wait** — Airdrops take months, sometimes years
