## Why Aave Matters

If you've spent any time in DeFi, you've probably heard of Aave. It's the largest decentralized lending protocol in crypto, with billions in total value locked across multiple chains. The core idea is simple: you deposit assets, earn yield from borrowers, and can borrow against your deposits if you want leverage.

Aave V3 specifically brought some important upgrades — efficiency mode for correlated assets, isolation mode for new tokens, and cross-chain portals. The fact that it deployed on MegaETH so early (the chain launched February 9) signals that MegaETH is being taken seriously by the big players. Chainlink's Scale program onboarding at launch made this possible, bringing the oracle infrastructure Aave needs to operate safely.

For my farming strategy, Aave is a no-brainer interaction. It's:

- **Battle-tested** — years of operation across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, and now MegaETH
- **Yield-generating** — my deposit earns interest passively while I sleep
- **A "blue chip" protocol** — exactly the kind of interaction that should register for any future MegaETH airdrop snapshot

## The Deposit

On February 10, one day after MegaETH mainnet went live, I supplied **5 USDT0** to Aave V3. The process was straightforward:

1. **Get USDT0** — I swapped 0.003 ETH for ~6.01 USDT0 on Kumbaya DEX
2. **Approve** — Granted Aave permission to spend exactly 5 USDT0 (I always approve exact amounts, never unlimited)
3. **Supply** — Deposited 5 USDT0 into the lending pool

Transaction: [`0x317c4409...77489c`](https://megaeth.blockscout.com/tx/0x317c4409589b71fa59399df90bca3e97411fd90b28f457023cb685102f77489c)

Gas cost? Essentially zero. MegaETH transactions are incredibly cheap, which makes micro-farming like this viable. On Ethereum mainnet, the gas for an Aave deposit would eat into a $5 position. Here, it's negligible.

## The Yield Angle

Stablecoin lending yields on Aave fluctuate based on utilization — how much of the pool is being borrowed. Early on a new chain, utilization can be volatile as liquidity builds up. The APY won't make me rich on a $5 deposit, but that's not really the point.

The point is **protocol interaction depth**. My deposit sits there, accruing aUSDT0 tokens (Aave's interest-bearing receipt tokens), generating on-chain activity that shows I'm not just a one-and-done farmer.
